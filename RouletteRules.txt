===================================================================
                       ROULETTE GAME RULES
===================================================================

Roulette is a classic casino game played with a wheel containing
37 numbered slots (0 to 36). The slots are colored as follows:
 - Number 0: Green (gives the House its mathematical edge)
 - Numbers 1-10 and 19-28: Odd numbers are RED, Even numbers are BLACK
 - Numbers 11-18 and 29-36: Even numbers are RED, Odd numbers are BLACK

-------------------------------------------------------------------
AVAILABLE BET TYPES & PAYOUTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------

1. SPECIFIC NUMBER (Straight Up Bet)
   - You place your bet on any single number from 0 to 36.
   - If the ball lands on your exact number, you win!
   - Payout: 35x your original bet.

2. COLOR (Red / Black Bet)
   - You choose whether the winning number will be Red or Black.
   - If the ball lands on 0 (Green), all Color bets lose.
   - Payout: 2x your original bet (Even money).

3. EVEN / ODD (Parity Bet)
   - You choose whether the winning number will be Even or Odd.
   - If the ball lands on 0 (Green), the House wins automatically,
     and all Even/Odd bets lose.
   - Payout: 2x your original bet (Even money).

-------------------------------------------------------------------
                            HOW TO PLAY
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Enter your bet amount (Must be equal to or higher than the minimum).
2. Choose your preferred bet type from the menu.
3. Enter your prediction based on the chosen bet type.
4. The dealer spins the wheel and drops the ball.
5. If your prediction matches the winning slot, your prize is
   calculated and added to your balance. Otherwise, the bet is lost.

                        Good luck at the table!
