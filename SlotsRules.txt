=================================================
           RULES FOR SLOTS
=================================================
1. Set your bet equal or above the minimum bet.
2. Spin to get identical symbols on the middle row!
3. Payouts (3 of a kind):
   - [777]             -> 20x your bet (JACKPOT)
   - [BAR]             -> 10x your bet
   - [PLUM / ORANGE]   -> 5x your bet
   - [CHERRY / LEMON]  -> 3x your bet
================================================
