===========================================================
                 RULES FOR BLACKJACK
===========================================================

1.The goal is to beat the dealer's hand without exceeding 21.
2.Face cards (J, Q, K) are worth 10 points.
3.Aces can count as 1 or 11 points, depending on your total.
4.You can choose to 'Hit' (draw a card) or 'Stand' (stop).
5.Dealer must hit until their cards total 17 or higher.
6.If you exceed 21, you 'Bust' and lose instantly.
7.A 'Push' occurs if totals match; your bet is returned.
